# Managing your website

This guide is for the person who runs the cafe. You do not need to know
anything technical. Everything on the website — the name, the photos, the menu,
the prices, the WhatsApp number — is changed from one place, called the admin
panel.

You can do all of it from your phone.

## 1. Signing in

1. Open your website and add `/admin` to the end of the address.
   So if your site is `example.com`, go to `example.com/admin`.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Tap **Sign in**.

You stay signed in on that phone or computer, so you will not have to do this
every time.

**Tip:** save `/admin` as a bookmark, or add it to your phone's home screen.

**If you forget your password**, contact whoever set up the site. There is no
"forgot password" link, on purpose — it keeps strangers out.

**Signing out:** tap the exit icon at the top right (or in the left menu on a
computer). Only do this if someone else uses your phone.

## 2. Changing a price

This is the most common thing you will do.

1. Sign in.
2. Tap **Menu** at the bottom of the screen.
3. Find the item and tap the **pencil** icon next to it.
4. Change the number in the **Price** box.
5. Tap **Save changes**.

The new price is on the website straight away. Refresh the page to see it.

Type the number only — `650`, not `Rs 650`. The website adds the currency
symbol for you.

## 3. Adding a new item

1. Tap **Menu**, then **New item**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Category** — which section it belongs to, like Burgers or Shakes.
   - **Name** — what customers will see.
   - **Description** — one short line. Can be left empty.
   - **Price** — numbers only.
   - **Sort order** — where it sits in its section. `1` is first, `2` second,
     and so on. Leave it at `0` if you do not mind.
   - **Item photo** — see below.
   - **Tags** — short words like `Spicy` or `Bestseller`, separated by commas.
     These show as small labels on the card. Can be left empty.
3. Leave **Available to order** switched on.
4. Tap **Add item**.

### Adding the photo

1. Tap **Upload photo**.
2. Choose a photo from your phone, or take one.
3. Wait for the spinner to finish. The photo appears in the small square.

Photos are shrunk automatically before uploading, so a large photo from your
phone camera is fine and will not slow the site down.

**Photo tips:** shoot from directly above or straight on, in daylight where you
can. Square or landscape photos look best. The card crops the photo to fit, so
keep the food in the middle.

## 4. Marking something sold out

When you run out of something, do not delete it. Turn it off instead.

1. Tap **Menu**.
2. Find the item.
3. Turn the **Available** switch off.

On the website the item goes grey, shows a **Sold out** label, and customers
cannot add it to their order. It is still there for you.

When it is back, turn the switch on again.

---

## 5. Choosing which items are featured

Featured items appear in the "Popular right now" row on the front page.

1. Tap **Menu**.
2. Turn the **Featured** switch on for the items you want to push.

Three to six is a good number. If you feature nothing, the row disappears from
the site, which is fine.

An item that is sold out will not show in the featured row even if it is
switched on.

## 6. Adding and reordering categories

Categories are the sections of the menu, like Ice Cream or Chinese.

1. Tap **Categories**.
2. To add one: type the name in the box at the top and tap **Add**.
3. To rename one: tap the **pencil**, change the name, tap the **tick**.
4. To change the order: use the **up** and **down** arrows on the left. This is
   the order customers see the tabs in.
5. To hide one without deleting it: turn its switch off.

**Careful:** deleting a category also deletes every item inside it, and their
photos. The site will ask you to confirm first. If you only want it off the
site for a while, turn the switch off instead.

## 7. Photos for the big slideshow

The slideshow is the large set of photos at the top of the front page. It
changes by itself every five seconds.

1. Tap **Carousel**.
2. Tap **New slide**.
3. Upload the photo.
4. **Title** and **Subtitle** are the words shown over the photo. Both can be
   left empty if the photo speaks for itself.
5. Tap **Add slide**.

To change the order, use the up and down arrows. To take a slide off the site
without deleting it, turn **Show this slide** off.

**Photo tips:** wide, landscape photos work best here. Anything too tall will
be cropped at the top and bottom. Avoid photos with important detail near the
middle, because the title sits there.

If you have no slides at all, the site shows your cafe name and tagline
instead. Nothing breaks.

## 8. Changing the WhatsApp number

1. Tap **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **WhatsApp number**.
3. Type the number in this exact style: country code, then the number, with
   **no plus sign, no spaces, and no zero at the start**.

   For a Pakistani number written as `0339 6789789`, you type `923396789789`.

4. Check the grey line underneath. It shows exactly where orders will go.
5. Tap **Save settings**.

If the number is in the wrong style, the page tells you and refuses to save. It
is protecting you: a wrong number means orders go nowhere.

**Leaving it empty** hides the WhatsApp buttons and shows a phone number to
call instead.

## 9. Putting a message at the top of the site

Use this for things like "Closed today" or "New winter menu".

1. Tap **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Announcement**.
3. Type your message in **Announcement text**.
4. Turn **Show the announcement bar** on.
5. Tap **Save settings**.

To remove it, turn the switch off and save. You do not need to delete the text
— it will be there next time you need it.

You cannot switch the bar on with no text; the page will stop you.

## 10. Changing the name, logo, address or hours

All of these live under **Settings**.

- **Cafe name** — appears at the top of the site, in the footer, in the browser
  tab, and in the WhatsApp message customers send you.
- **Tagline** — the small line under the name.
- **Logo** — tap **Upload photo**. A square image works best. Uploading a new
  one replaces the old one.
- **Phone shown on the site** — what customers see and tap to call. Write it
  however you like, for example `0339 6789789`.
- **Address** — this also moves the map pin and the directions button. Write it
  the way Google Maps would recognise it.
- **Opening hours** — free text, for example `Daily, 12pm to 12am`.
- **Currency symbol** — appears before every price.
- **Instagram and Facebook links** — paste the full web address. Leave empty to
  hide that icon.
- **Order greeting** — the first line of the WhatsApp message customers send.

Under **Ordering** there is a grey box headed **Customers will send**. That is
a live preview of the exact message a customer sends you. Change the greeting
or currency and watch it update before you save.

Always tap **Save settings** at the bottom.

## 11. How customers order

There is no online payment. Orders arrive as WhatsApp messages.

1. The customer browses the menu and taps **Add** on what they want.
2. A bar appears at the bottom showing their total.
3. They tap it, check the list, and tap **Send order on WhatsApp**.
4. WhatsApp opens on their phone with the order already written out.
5. They press send, and it arrives on your WhatsApp.

The message contains each item, the quantity, the prices, the total, and blank
lines for their name and whether they want pickup or delivery.

You confirm the order by replying in WhatsApp as normal.

**Worth doing:** place a test order from your own phone once a month, so you
know it still works.

## 12. When something looks wrong

**I changed something but the website looks the same.**
Refresh the page. On a phone, pull down from the top. If it still looks old,
close the tab and open it again.

**A photo will not upload.**
Check you have signal. Try a smaller photo. If it keeps failing, you may have
been signed out — go to `/admin` and sign in again.

**It says my session expired.**
Sign in again. This happens if you have not used the panel for a long time.

**I deleted something by accident.**
It cannot be undone. Add it again. This is why sold-out items should be
switched off rather than deleted.

**The website will not load at all.**
The site and the menu are hosted separately from your WhatsApp, so orders by
phone still work. Contact whoever set the site up.

## A short checklist before you share the link

- Every item has the correct price
- Every item has a real photo
- Names are spelled the way you say them in the shop
- Opening hours match reality
- The map pin is on your actual door
- Your logo is uploaded
- At least three slideshow photos are added
- You have placed a test order from your own phone and received it
